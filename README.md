# Morphostructure of the Pacific Deep-Sea Trenches (seminar, in Russian)

LaTeX (Beamer) source and compiled PDF of the seminar presentation
**"Morphostructure Features of the Deep-Sea Trenches in the Pacific Ocean: The
Problem of Their Origin"** by Polina Lemenkova (delivered in Russian).

Presented at the **Seminar of the Laboratory of Regional Geophysics and Natural
Disasters, Schmidt Institute of Physics of the Earth, Russian Academy of Sciences
(IFZ RAN), Moscow, on 24 May 2021**.

The 79-slide talk examines the submarine morphostructure and geomorphometry of
the Pacific deep-sea trenches and the question of their origin, with a
trench-by-trench treatment (Kuril-Kamchatka, Aleutian and others) based on
bathymetric and geophysical data modelling.
